🚀 AI-Powered Lead Classification & Sales Routing Automation

n8n · personal project
  • Designed and built an AI-powered lead management automation using n8n, Google Gemini, Gmail, Slack, and structured data processing.
  • The system automatically captures new leads from a form, analyzes the customer's inquiry using an AI Classification Agent, and categorizes the lead into Homeowner, Contractor, Designer, Builder, or Unknown.
  • A structured output parser ensures the AI returns consistent classification data, including the customer type, confidence level, and reasoning. The classified lead is then routed through a Switch node to the appropriate salesperson based on customer type.
  • The workflow automatically generates a professional internal follow-up email containing the customer's contact information, classification details, inquiry, and recommended follow-up action, delivered directly to the assigned salesperson.
  • The system also sends a follow-up notification through Slack, providing the sales team with an additional channel for lead monitoring and follow-up.
  • This automation demonstrates AI-powered lead qualification, conditional routing, structured AI outputs, automated sales assignment, email generation, team notifications, and human-in-the-loop sales processes.
Form submission → Classification Agent (Gemini) → Switch routing → Email + Slack

Key capabilities

  • 🤖AI Qualification — Automatically understands and categorizes customer inquiries.
  • 🔀Intelligent Routing — Sends each lead to the appropriate salesperson.
  • ✉️Automated Communication — Generates professional internal sales notifications.
  • 💬Team Visibility — Notifies the sales team through Slack.
  • 🛡️Error Handling — Uses a separate workflow to capture, log, and report automation failures.
  • 👨‍💼Human-in-the-Loop — AI handles qualification and routing while the salesperson handles the actual customer relationship.
Technology Stack: n8n Google Gemini Gmail Slack Telegram Google Sheets AI Agents Structured Output Parser Switch/Conditional Routing Error Trigger

🌤️ AI-Powered Weather Monitoring, Reporting & Error-Handling System

n8n · personal project
  • Designed and built an end-to-end automated weather monitoring system using n8n, OpenWeatherMap API, AI Agents, Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram, Docker, and Tailscale.
  • The system automatically retrieves weather data on a scheduled basis, processes the information through an AI-powered Weather Assistant, generates a human-readable forecast with practical recommendations, delivers the report through Gmail, and records the weather data in Google Sheets.

  • The solution demonstrates API integration, AI automation, scheduled workflows, multi-service orchestration, data logging, notification systems, error handling, and automation monitoring.
6:00 AM schedule → OpenWeatherMap API → AI Weather Assistant → Gmail + Sheets → Telegram alerts
